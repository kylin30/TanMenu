## TanMenu 0.15.0

### 中文

本版全面重构 TanMenu 的界面与交互，在保留启动器“一次点击打开应用”核心体验的同时，统一为更轻量、清晰的现代设计。

- 采用固定左侧导航与右侧内容布局，应用、设置和退出入口更直接。
- 应用页仍一次显示全部应用，搜索、主目录和刷新集中在顶部操作区。
- 重新设计分类面板、应用按钮、悬停反馈、间距和尺寸，提升信息密度与一致性。
- 设置页改为连续显示全部设置组，点击左侧目录即可滚动定位，不再切换和隐藏内容。
- 统一设置卡片、输入框、开关和按钮样式，并精简说明文本，减少不必要的换行。
- 使用纯色窗口背景，优化不同缩放比例下的布局和对齐。

### English

This release comprehensively redesigns TanMenu's UI and interactions while preserving its core one-click app-launching workflow.

- A persistent left navigation rail provides direct access to Apps, Settings, and Exit.
- The Apps page still shows every application at once, with search, root-folder, and refresh actions grouped at the top.
- Category panels, app buttons, hover feedback, spacing, and sizing have been redesigned for higher information density and consistency.
- Settings now appear as one continuous page; selecting a section scrolls to it instead of switching or hiding content.
- Settings cards, fields, switches, and buttons share a unified visual system, with shorter guidance text to avoid unnecessary wrapping.
- A solid window background and refined alignment improve the experience across display scales.
